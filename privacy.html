Privacy Policy

AI Data Workflows
Effective date: November 14, 2026

This policy explains how the privately operated AI Data Workflows project handles information when its maintainer uses the workflow with their own accounts. The project is intended for personal use, not as a public service for other users.

Information accessed

When authorized by the account owner, the workflow uses the Google Drive API to find, download, organize, and upload files in that owner's Google Drive. Files may include data exports and processed datasets used in the owner's data workflows. The workflow may process information contained in those files, including job-posting details, descriptions, company names, and application links.

Google OAuth credentials and access tokens authenticate the owner's requests. They are intended to be stored locally or in protected automation secrets and are not published in this public repository.

How information is used

Information accessed through Google Drive is used to run the owner's requested file-management, data-ingestion, transformation, deduplication, and reporting workflows. The project does not operate a user database or provide the owner's files to other users.

Storage and sharing

Source files and generated outputs remain in the owner's Google Drive, subject to the owner's Drive settings. If automated runs are configured, authentication material may be stored as protected repository or workflow secrets with the automation provider. The project owner does not sell personal information or share Drive file contents for advertising.

Third-party services

The workflow may interact with Google Drive and, when configured by the owner, external data-collection providers such as Apify and automation hosting such as GitHub Actions. Those services process information under their own terms and privacy policies. This project does not control their independent practices.

Data retention and deletion

The owner controls retention and deletion of files in their Google Drive and removal of credentials from their local environment or automation settings. Revoking the application's Google account access through Google Account security settings prevents future authorized access.

Security

Reasonable steps are taken to keep OAuth credentials out of public source control and restrict access to the owner's automation environment. No method of electronic storage or transmission is guaranteed to be completely secure.

Children's privacy

This personal project is not directed to children and is not intended for use by children.

Changes and contact

This policy may be updated if the workflow's data practices change. For questions, contact the project maintainer through the GitHub profile associated with this project.
